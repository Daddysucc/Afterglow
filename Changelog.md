# Changelog

What's new in each version, newest first: a few short lines each (see `CLAUDE.md`).

## 3.8.0

- The sky can show the weather where you are (optional).
- Wallpaper changes are smaller, and wait until you won't see them.
- The sky follows the sun's real height where you are.

## 3.7.0

- Clearer settings, and numbers you can type in.
- Updates show what's new; half the memory in the tray.
- The accent color follows the sky, and only changes unseen.

## 3.6.5

- Uninstalling or turning the wallpaper off restores yours properly.
- Fewer wake-ups during long transitions.

## 3.6.4

- The accent color only changes while you're away.

## 3.6.3

- No more stalls when changing the time of day.

## 3.6.2

- Smoother sky changes.

## 3.6.1

- Wallpaper and accent changes wait for quiet moments.

## 3.6.0

- Dynamic wallpaper, with a backlight-matching accent color.
- Hue lights match the screen more closely.

## 3.5.3

- A Forget bridge button for Hue.

## 3.5.2

- Fixed pairing with a Hue bridge.

## 3.5.1

- New icon.

## 3.5.0

- Philips Hue lights follow the color cycle.
- Each settings tab can be reset on its own.

## 3.4.0

- Gentler transitions.

## 3.3.1

- "Reset all settings" moved where it's harder to hit by accident.

## 3.3.0

- Choose how often to check for updates.
- Starts about twice as fast.

## 3.2.0

- LightBulb is now Afterglow, and updates install themselves.

## 3.0.1

- A "Check now" button for updates.

## 3.0.0

- New dashboard, evening stage, and grayscale mode.
- Per-display settings, streaming pause, and an app whitelist.
